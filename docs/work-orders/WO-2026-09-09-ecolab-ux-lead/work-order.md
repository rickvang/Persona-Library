## Integrity and production decisions

- ATS-first output selected; no separate human-facing resume was created for this role.
- The active master resume was copied before tailoring; the active master and reusable cover-letter master remain unchanged.
- Unsupported ServiceNow, UI Builder, Now Experience, ServiceNow Mobile, certification, direct usability-testing, WCAG 2.1, Storybook, design-token, and executive-leadership claims were excluded.
- The cover letter was manually reviewed for generic or AI-like phrasing; this was not an automated detector or employer-specific test.
- The cover letter uses concrete evidence and names the ServiceNow-specific ramp-up instead of implying prior platform experience.
- Workflow learning: the candidate revision showed that cover letters need a positioning layer between the evidence ledger and sendable prose. Candidate-owned abstractions and voice edits should pass when they preserve material meaning; only new material facts or changes to scope, ownership, metrics, tools, credentials, dates, or gaps require evidence escalation.
- No application was submitted, no employer was contacted, and no packet was externally shared.
