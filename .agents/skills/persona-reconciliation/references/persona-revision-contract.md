# Persona revision contract

For an authorized change, preserve the existing Persona and make the smallest supported update. Record the affected fields, evidence, classification, confidence change, source/resource contribution, semantic rationale, date or revision context, and remaining uncertainty according to the current repository schema.

Keep required updates separate from optional improvements and checked-but-unchanged fields. A contradiction remains visible until an authorized decision resolves it; do not silently overwrite the older claim. A new conclusion supersedes prior rationale through the repository's append-oriented revision mechanism rather than erasing history.

The adapter prepares and validates a scoped update set. It does not grant authority, execute Tools, update unrelated records, or replace the universal reconciliation pass. After the adapter, run one universal `$change-impact-reconciliation` review when the initiating contract requires it.
