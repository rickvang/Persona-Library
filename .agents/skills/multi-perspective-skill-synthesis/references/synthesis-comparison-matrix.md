## How to use it

1. Fill each perspective independently and cite the source or record for material claims.
2. Put a claim in **Shared core** only when at least two perspectives converge on portable judgment, not just the same noun.
3. Put context, priorities, protections, and different thresholds in **Role-specific application** unless evidence proves they are universal.
4. Put conflicts and absent evidence in a separate unresolved list; do not resolve them by averaging.
5. Use the final column to distinguish observed, documented, inferred, proposed, and unknown claims.

The matrix should produce a decision about the smallest stable artifact: existing Skill, relationship, primitive, composed Skill, application, workflow method, Tool-use recipe, or no new Skill.
