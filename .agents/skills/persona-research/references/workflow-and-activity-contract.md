# Workflow and activity contract

## Workflow

A workflow is a meaningful trigger-to-outcome path. Capture, when supported:

- trigger and desired outcome;
- steps, decisions, and handoffs;
- collaborators, tools, information, and workarounds;
- friction, risks, protections, and ranked concerns;
- success signals or quality standards;
- related activities, evidence, confidence, and product implications.

Use `foundational`, `supporting`, and `edge-case` tiers only when the evidence supports the distinction. Model multiple workflows when the role's paths materially differ; do not force every Persona to contain every tier.

## Activity

An activity is a recurring or interrupt-driven unit of work. Capture cadence or estimated duration, trigger, planned/reactive/interrupt-driven status, collaborators, tools and information needed, desired outcome, friction, and related workflow or journey. Do not collapse an activity list into workflow steps: activities may recur across workflows and should remain independently inspectable.

## Priorities and inference

Separate stated priorities, observed behavior protections, and research hypotheses. A workflow concern is not automatically a user request, and a named tool is not automatically available. When a source names only a category or the research implies a likely tool, label it as reported or representative hypothesis and include a validation note.
