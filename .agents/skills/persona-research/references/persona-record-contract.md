# Persona record contract

This reference explains the concepts the research skill must preserve. The repository's current Persona record and validator remain authoritative for exact property names, ordering, and required values. If the record and this reference differ, follow the record and report the discrepancy.

## Record-level concepts

Capture only what the evidence supports:

- role and relevant industry or product context;
- geography, audience, timeframe, lifecycle, operating context, and operating state;
- goals, constraints, needs, decision pressures, and confidence;
- journeys or workflows and their linked activities;
- portable skills and their evidence or quality signals;
- tool candidates with an explicit evidence status;
- resources, open questions, validation activities, and revision context.

Do not turn the Persona into a biography. A Persona is a structured model of operating conditions and behavior. A representative example can explain a pattern, but it must not be presented as a real observed individual unless the evidence supports that claim.

## Record-writing boundary

Research-only work produces a proposal or brief. A live record may be created or changed only when the user explicitly authorizes it, identifies the target or destination, and the change is scoped and validated. Do not silently replace an existing record or rewrite unrelated fields. After an authorized Persona change, use the `persona-reconciliation` adapter and then one universal `$change-impact-reconciliation` pass.
