# Quality signal patterns

Quality signals should be observable and tied to the capability's intended use. Useful patterns include:

- decision quality: relevant constraints identified, alternatives compared, rationale traceable;
- execution quality: steps are complete, assumptions visible, handoffs usable, output meets the stated contract;
- evidence quality: sources are in scope, claims are labeled, uncertainty and limitations remain visible;
- adaptation quality: the capability survives realistic context changes without losing its boundary;
- safety quality: permissions, Tool availability, authority, and failure recovery are not guessed.

Each signal should have an inspection method, at least one realistic variation or counterexample, and a known failure mode when that information is available. These are patterns, not universal acceptance thresholds.
