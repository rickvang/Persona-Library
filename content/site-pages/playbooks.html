Process, people, and evidence

A Playbook supplies a reusable process. Its operating Persona coordinates the work; specialists own their domain judgment. Tools retain their own access and permission boundaries.

Participants

Use existing Personas with bounded responsibilities. Participation does not grant mutation authority.

Shared state

The actual request, source evidence, artifact versions, decisions, and handoffs belong to the run, not the reusable catalog.

Change control

Change the owning process document first. Catalog previews and readers are derived from that source; reconciliation checks the affected relationships.

Execution boundary

A source outline explains the intended process. It does not establish which records were loaded, which route was followed, or whether a quality check ran.

Operating Packs supply context; Tools supply acting capabilities; Docs explain the contracts.
