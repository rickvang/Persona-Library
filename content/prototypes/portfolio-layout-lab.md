# Portfolio Layout Lab

- Prototype id: `proto-portfolio-layout-lab`
- Status: Active / Explore
- Mode: Compare and select
- Boundary: Prototype-only; no live Portfolio or Persona Library records depend on this artifact.
- Audience: Hiring managers and design leadership first; potential consulting clients second.
- Stable content fixture:
  - Positioning: “I build the conditions for better work.”
  - Primary case study: Multi Product Integrations.
  - Future case studies: AI Systems; UI Design Practices.
  - Note: Personas as a design input and conversational research partner.
  - Primary action: Explore selected work.
- Shared success criteria:
  1. A hiring reader understands the point of view and finds evidence quickly.
  2. A consulting reader can understand the kinds of complexity Rick helps make clearer.
  3. Navigation remains legible on desktop and becomes a usable drawer on mobile where appropriate.
  4. Motion supports narrative and hierarchy without delaying content or breaking reduced-motion preferences.
  5. The layout preserves realistic content, case-study depth, and clear next actions.

## Options

### A — Cinematic left rail

Persistent desktop rail with numbered sections, orange accent, and mobile drawer. Tests whether a strong editorial frame creates memorable orientation without hiding work.

### B — Compact top navigation

Traditional top bar with a large hero and work grid. Tests whether familiar navigation increases findability and keeps the portfolio feeling lighter.

### C — Split editorial index

A sticky section index paired with a continuously changing work stage. Tests whether the information architecture can feel like a guided editorial table of contents.

### D — Case-study deck

A mostly immersive story surface with restrained navigation and chapter markers. Tests whether a narrative-first portfolio increases case-study engagement at the cost of global orientation.
